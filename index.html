04 · FIELD NOTES · SONOMA 2026

What we heard, row after row.

A week across Sonoma — growers, vineyard managers, UC Davis researchers, and association leaders. One consistent theme.

01
Economics
Margins are thinning. Tariffs, drought, and wildfire smoke — arriving at once, with less room to recover from a bad call.
02
Labour
~40% of vineyard cost. Skilled crews are scarce and expensive. The waste is walking rows that don't need the attention.
03
Yield uncertainty
Crop estimates drive labour, fruit contracts, harvest logistics, and winery plans. Late info costs more to fix.
04
Climate & disease
Red blotch, powdery mildew, frost, smoke — sometimes in one season. Pressures compound. A late-May frost can cut yield 25%.
05
Tool adoption
Only simple tools survive. A phone, a camera on an ATV, a sensor that just runs. No new workflow earns a place in the season.
